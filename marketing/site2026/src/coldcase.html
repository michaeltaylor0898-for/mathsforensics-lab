Questions about Cold Case

Are these past papers?

No. Every Cold Case is an original paper written for Maths Forensics, so nobody has seen the questions in class and the score is an honest one.

Which exam boards and tier?

GCSE Maths Higher, written to the AQA and Edexcel specifications. They also suit OCR and Cambridge IGCSE Extended students as practice.

How do I get the papers?

Straight after you pay, you get an email with your login link. The papers and mark schemes are PDFs you can print as many times as you like. Read "Start here" before you print anything.

Which should I buy?

Cold Case Complete gives you three full mocks to spread across the year. Add Most Wanted if you're aiming for a grade 8 or 9. If you'd like your papers marked for you, the Exam Season Kit includes all of them.
