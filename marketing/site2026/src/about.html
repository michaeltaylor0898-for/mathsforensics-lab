About Maths Forensics

A maths teacher who got tired of watching marks go missing.

I'm Mike Taylor. I've taught GCSE and IGCSE maths for over a decade and marked thousands of exam papers. Every summer I watched capable students walk out of the exam having lost marks on maths they knew. Maths Forensics is what I built to stop that.

How it started

So I looked at the evidence.

I took 280 anonymised GCSE maths scripts and coded every lost mark by its cause. Two-thirds were topics the student hadn't secured yet. But nearly one in three were on maths they already knew: misread questions, slips, and working that was never shown.

That meant two jobs, not one. Build the maths properly, and protect the marks students have already earned. No single website did both, so I started building one.

What we believe

Four rules we build by.

Tested in a real classroom first.

Every game, question and feature goes in front of my own Year 7 and Year 9 classes before anyone else sees it. If it doesn't work on a Tuesday afternoon, it doesn't ship.

Strict marking is kind marking.

No method seen, no method mark. It's stricter than the exam, so students learn to protect every mark before it counts.

No grade promises.

Results depend on the student. We promise a clear diagnosis and a clear next step, and a refund if it doesn't help.

Children's data is protected.

No adverts, no tracking and no messaging between users. Photos of working are deleted after 7 days. Student privacy
