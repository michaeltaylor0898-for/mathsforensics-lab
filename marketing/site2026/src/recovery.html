Good to know

Questions about the Mark Recovery System

When does it open?

On 6 January, in time for mocks and the run-up to the summer exams. Reserve your place and you'll be told the moment it opens.

What's a Casefile?

A complete guide to one topic area: the key ideas in clear explanation boxes, worked examples, the misconceptions that lose marks, a Knowledge Check and full solutions.

How is it different from the Lab?

The Lab is online practice for every topic, with feedback and marking. The Mark Recovery System is a course: it teaches the technique and the topics, and the Tracker shows you which to fix first. They work well together.

Is it part of The Full Investigation?

Yes. The Full Investigation includes the Mark Recovery System and the Exam Execution Guide, with the Lab, Cold Case papers and Forensic Marking.
