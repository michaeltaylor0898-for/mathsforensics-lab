Questions schools ask

Good to know

What devices does it work on?

Anything with a modern browser: Chromebooks, laptops, iPads, Android tablets, phones and the classroom board. Nothing to install.

How do students sign in?

With their school Google or Microsoft account, or an email and password. They join your class with its code.

Which exam boards does the Lab cover?

The GCSE maths content set by the Department for Education, which AQA, Edexcel and OCR all teach, plus Cambridge IGCSE.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is charged. You choose whether to continue with a school licence.

Can one teacher start without a school licence?

Yes. Teacher Pro is £49 a year for one teacher and covers unlimited live Incident Room games with your classes. Sign in at the Incident Room to start a free 14-day trial. If your school later buys a licence, every teacher is covered.

Can parents buy it for home as well?

Yes, an Academy Pass or a GCSE plan. Home accounts are kept separate from school, and a teacher never switches off something a family has paid for.
