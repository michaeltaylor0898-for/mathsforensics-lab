Good to know

Questions about the Jumpstart Lab

Who is it for?

Students who've finished GCSE maths and are starting A-level maths, or who've started Year 12 and are finding it harder than expected. It suits anyone aiming for a steady start rather than a stressful one.

When should they start?

Ideally the summer after GCSEs, so they arrive in September ready. If Year 12 has already started, start now: the diagnostic shows which gaps are slowing them down, and the modules fix those first.

How much time does it take?

About 2 to 3 hours a week for six weeks. The six-week schedule can be followed exactly or squeezed, focusing on the modules the Gap Map flags.

Does it teach the A-level course in advance?

No, and it doesn't replace school lessons. It secures the GCSE foundations every A-level course assumes, then gives a first taste of A-level thinking so the first lessons feel familiar.

Which exam board does it follow?

The foundations it covers are the same for AQA, Edexcel and OCR A-level maths, so it works whichever board the school uses.
