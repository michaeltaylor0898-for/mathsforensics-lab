The Incident Room

A maths knockout your class will talk about at lunch.

Show the game on the board. Students join on tablets with a 6-digit code. Everyone plays the qualifiers, the top eight go through to the quarter-finals, and the rest play on in the Reserve League and predict the winners.

  • KS3 and GCSE versions
  • Run it in one lesson, or as homework over a week
  • Every game ends with a debrief of every mistake
