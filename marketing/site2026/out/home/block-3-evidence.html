The evidence

Nearly 1 in 3 lost marks were on maths the student already knew.

We analysed 280 GCSE maths scripts and coded every lost mark by its cause. Two-thirds were topics not yet secure. The rest were misreads, slips and missing working: marks that were already earned, then given away.
