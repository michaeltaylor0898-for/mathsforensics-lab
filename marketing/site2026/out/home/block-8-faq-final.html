Questions parents ask

Good to know

Is my child ready for the Academy or the Lab?

Years 7 to 9 start in the Academy, which builds KS3 maths step by step. Years 10 and 11, or anyone sitting GCSE or IGCSE this year, start in the Lab. They share one sign-in, so moving up later is easy.

Is it a subscription?

No. The Academy Pass is one payment for 12 months. GCSE plans are one payment that lasts until the summer 2027 exams. There's nothing to cancel.

Which exam boards does it cover?

The Lab follows the GCSE maths content that AQA, Edexcel and OCR all teach, plus Cambridge IGCSE. Cold Case papers are Higher tier, written for AQA and Edexcel.

What is Forensic Marking?

Your child writes their working on screen or photographs it on paper. It's marked mark by mark, with the line that earned or lost each mark and how to fix it. Photos are deleted after 7 days and the feedback stays in their report. How AI marking works

How does my child sign in?

Buy with the email your child will use. GCSE students sign in with Google, Microsoft or email. For the Academy, you create the account as the parent and add each child with their own PIN.

Is it safe for children?

There are no adverts, no tracking and no messaging between users. We collect the minimum needed and follow the ICO's Children's Code. Student privacy · Safeguarding

What if it doesn't help?

GCSE plans come with a 30-day money-back guarantee. Use it with your child's next practice paper, and if it doesn't improve their approach, ask for a refund.
