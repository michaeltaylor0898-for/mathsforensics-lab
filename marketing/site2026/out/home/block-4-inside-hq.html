Other platforms tell a student what they got wrong. HQ works out why, and what to do about it.

Exhibit A

It names the mistake, not just the score.

Every wrong answer in the Academy and the Lab is matched to the error behind it. A student who adds the denominators is told exactly that, and shown how to fix it, before the habit sets.

A typical platform: "Incorrect. The answer is 7/12." HQ: "You added the denominators. Make them the same first."
Lab · Fractions · Q4Practise

Work out 1/3 + 1/4

Your answer: 2/7✗
The mistakeYou added the tops and the bottoms. Change both to twelfths first: 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12.
Exhibit B

It marks the working the way an examiner does.

Forensic Marking reads handwritten working, from a stylus or a photo, and awards every method and accuracy mark with the line that earned it. Mike mode watches as the student writes and warns at the exact step where students usually slip.

Our rule: no method seen, no method mark. It's stricter than the exam, so students learn to protect every mark.
