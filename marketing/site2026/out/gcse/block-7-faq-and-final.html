Good to know

How long does the Exam Pass last?

Until 16 July 2027, after the summer 2027 exams. It's one payment, not a subscription, so there's nothing to cancel.

Which exams is it for?

GCSE maths for AQA, Edexcel and OCR, and Cambridge IGCSE, up to Higher tier. Cold Case papers are Higher tier, written for AQA and Edexcel.

I'm a parent. How does my child get in?

Buy with the email your child will sign in with. They sign in with Google, Microsoft or email, and the Exam Pass is already unlocked.

Should we get the Lab or the Exam Execution Guide?

The Guide teaches how to sit the paper. The Lab is where you practise every topic. If marks go on questions you can already do, start with the Guide. If topics aren't secure yet, start with the Lab. The Full Investigation includes both.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes, in the browser on a phone, tablet or laptop. Nothing to install. A stylus helps with Forensic Marking, but a finger or a photo of paper works too.

My school uses something else. Can I still use the Lab?

Yes. The Exam Pass is yours, not your school's, and works alongside whatever your school sets.
