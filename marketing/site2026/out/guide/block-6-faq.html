Good to know

Questions about the Guide

Who is it for?

GCSE and IGCSE maths students, Foundation or Higher, especially those who know more than their marks show: in Year 10 or 11, or resitting.

Does it teach the maths topics?

No. It teaches how to sit the paper. The topics are taught in the Mark Recovery System's Casefiles and practised in the Lab.

How long does it take?

It's deliberately short, so it can be used straight away: watch a lesson, then apply it to your next practice paper with the printable tools.

Should we get the Guide or the Lab?

The Guide teaches technique; the Lab is where you practise every topic. If marks go on questions you can already do, start with the Guide.
