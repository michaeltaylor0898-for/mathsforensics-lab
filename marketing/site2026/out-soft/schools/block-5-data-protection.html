Data protection

Built for UK GDPR from day one.

Designed with children's data in mind, following the ICO's Children's Code. Your school stays in control of pupil data.

  • Your school is the data controller; we process pupil data only on your instructions, under a data processing agreement
  • Minimum data: a name, a school sign-in and progress, stored in the EU
  • No adverts, no tracking and no messaging between users
  • Pupil data deleted on request
  • A UK representative appointed under UK GDPR
