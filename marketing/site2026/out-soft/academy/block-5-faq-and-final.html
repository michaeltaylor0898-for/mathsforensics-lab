Questions parents ask

Good to know

Which years is the Academy for?

Years 7, 8 and 9 (KS3). If your child is in Year 10 or 11, or sitting GCSE this year, the Lab is the right place.

How does my child sign in?

You buy the pass and create a parent account with the same email. Add your child, choose a PIN, and they sign in with their name and PIN at lab.mathsforensics.com.

I have two children. Do I need two passes?

Yes, a pass unlocks one child. Both children can sit under the same parent account, so you see them side by side.

Can my child try it first?

Yes. Several topics are free to try, with no payment and no account needed. Try free topics

Does it work on a tablet or phone?

Yes. It runs in the browser on a tablet, phone or laptop, with on-screen keys for fractions, negatives and powers. There's nothing to install.

My child's school uses something else. Can we still use it?

Yes. A home account is separate from school, and works alongside whatever their school sets.
