Questions schools ask

Good to know

What devices does it work on?

Anything with a modern web browser: Chromebooks, laptops, iPads and Android tablets, phones and the classroom board. Nothing to install.

How do students sign in?

With their school Google or Microsoft account, or an email and password. They join your class with its 6-letter code.

Which exam boards does the Lab cover?

The Lab follows the GCSE maths subject content set by the Department for Education, which AQA, Edexcel and OCR all teach.

Can parents buy it for home as well?

Yes. Parents can buy an Academy Pass or a Lab Exam Pass for their child. Home accounts are kept separate from school, and a teacher never switches off something a family has paid for.

When can we start?

Today, with the free trial. Full school licences start from January 2027.
