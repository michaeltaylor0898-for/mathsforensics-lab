How every topic works

Learn it. Practise it. Play it. Prove it.

The same four steps for all 78 topics, so your child always knows what to do next, even without you there.

01 · LEARN

See it done

A worked example that reveals one step at a time, then "Try one with me": a question broken into small steps they answer themselves.

02 · PRACTISE

Feedback on every answer

Questions at three levels, with hints when they're stuck. A wrong answer gets told what went wrong, not just "incorrect".

03 · PLAY

Training games

Games built for the topic, like Departures (timetables and time) and Evidence Locker (collecting like terms).

04 · CHECK

Secure the topic

Five questions, no hints. Four right and the topic is secured, ticked off on their pathway.

Questions parents ask

Good to know

Which year groups is it for?

Years 7, 8 and 9 (ages 11 to 14). It follows the KS3 national curriculum for England, so it also suits international schools that teach the English curriculum.

Does my child need their own email?

No. You create the account with your email and add your child with their first name and year group. They train through your account.

What do they need to use it?

Any device with a web browser: laptop, tablet, Chromebook or phone. Nothing to install.

I have two children. Do I need two passes?

Each Academy Pass covers one child for 12 months. Both children can sit under the same parent account, each with their own progress.

Their school already uses Maths Forensics. Do we need a pass?

If their teacher has opened the Academy for their class, they can use it with their school login, at school and at home. A home pass is separate: it sits under your account, with its own progress.

Is it a subscription?

No. The Academy Pass is one payment for 12 months. It doesn't renew on its own.
