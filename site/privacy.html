Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

The short version
  • We collect only what the Lab needs: your name, email address and your answers to questions.
  • We use it to run the Lab: marking, progress, recommendations and, for school students, teacher reports.
  • We never sell your data, never show adverts and don't use tracking or advertising cookies.
  • Your data is stored in the UK (London).
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time: email support@mathsforensics.com.

1. Who we are

Maths Forensics Lab ("the Lab", "we", "us") is an online GCSE maths practice service at lab.mathsforensics.com, run by Maths Forensics (Michael Robert Taylor). Contact: support@mathsforensics.com. Postal address: Calle Cervantes 38 DCHA 5, Madrid 28014 Spain.

Who is responsible for your data depends on how you use the Lab:

  • Exam Pass and free accounts: Maths Forensics is the data controller. We decide how your data is used, as described here.
  • School accounts (students who joined with a class code, teachers and school admins): your school is the data controller and Maths Forensics is a data processor, acting on the school's instructions under our agreement with the school. Questions about how your school uses the Lab should go to your school first. We will help them answer.

4. Children

The Lab is designed for GCSE students, usually aged 14 to 16, and we follow the principles of the UK Age Appropriate Design Code: we collect the minimum data, have no adverts or tracking, keep privacy-protective settings by default and don't share data for marketing. Students under 13 should only use the Lab through their school or with a parent or carer setting up the account. Parents and carers can contact us to see, correct or delete their child's data.

6. How long we keep it

  • School accounts: for as long as the school's licence is active. When a licence ends and isn't renewed, we delete or return the school's data within 90 days, or sooner if the school asks.
  • Exam Pass and free accounts: while your account is in use. We delete accounts that have not been used for 2 years. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.
  • Purchase records: 6 years, because tax law requires it.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.

You can also clear your own answers at any time from the Case file page ("Clear my case file").

7. Keeping it safe

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored securely hashed by our sign-in provider. We can't see them.
  • Database rules make sure each person can only see the data they are allowed to: students their own, teachers their own classes, school admins their own school.
  • If a data breach ever affects you, we will tell you (and your school, and the regulator where required) without undue delay.

8. Cookies and browser storage

The Lab does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It saves a few items in your browser's local storage so it works properly:

  • Your sign-in session, so you stay signed in (essential).
  • Your preferences: whether the exam timer is on, whether you have hidden the guide, which Case briefings you have already seen.

These stay on your device. Signing out removes your session; clearing your browser data removes the rest.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • have it corrected if it's wrong;
  • have it deleted;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • receive your data in a format you can take elsewhere.

Email support@mathsforensics.com. We reply within one month. If you use the Lab through your school, we may pass your request to the school, as it is responsible for that data.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or, in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es). We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will show a notice in the Lab and email account holders and schools before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.
