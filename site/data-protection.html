For parents

The Academy (Years 7 to 9) at home

  • You own the account. You sign up with your email address and add your child as a profile. Children can't create their own account at home, and they don't need an email address.
  • What we keep about your child: the first name you give (up to 20 characters), their school year, an optional 4-digit PIN (stored scrambled, so we can't read it), which topics they've learned, practised and secured, and any certificates they've earned.
  • Who sees it: you, and your child when they use the profile. Nobody else: other families and other children never see it.
  • Deleting it: remove a child's profile from your account at any time and their progress goes with it. You can also ask us to delete your whole account.

The Lab (GCSE) and Forensic Marking

  • Students have their own sign-in. We keep their name, email address and answers, so the Lab can mark work and track progress.
  • If a student uses written answers, an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) reads a picture of their maths working to mark it. It never receives the student's name or email, and the provider may not use it to train its models. The picture is deleted after 7 days; the marks and feedback stay in the student's record. How AI marking works.
  • The weekly report and mock analyses are built from the student's own answers, and only the student (and their teacher, for school classes) can see them.

Emails

We send essential account emails. Marketing emails go only to adults who have agreed to them, with an unsubscribe link in every email. We never send marketing to children.

For schools

QuestionAnswer
Who is the data controller?For school accounts, the school is the controller. HARLEQUIN DIGITAL INNOVATIONS, S.L. (trading as Maths Forensics) is the processor and acts only on the school's instructions.
Is there a data processing agreement?Yes. Every school licence includes our data processing agreement (Article 28, UK GDPR and EU GDPR). Email us for a copy to review before a pilot.
What data do you process?Teachers: name, school email, classes. Students: name, school email or a class sign-in, year group, answers, progress, set work and (Lab only, if used) pictures of written working. No special category data.
Where is it stored?In the EU (Ireland). Providers outside the UK and EU are covered by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Full list below.
Who can see a student's data?The student, their own teachers and the school's admins. Database rules enforce this: teachers see only their own classes. Students never see each other's data.
Is AI used on students' work?Only in the Lab's Forensic Marking, which schools can switch on as a paid add-on. The AI sees the picture of working and the question, never names or emails. It's off for schools unless you choose it.
SecurityEncrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, row-level access rules in the database, and Google or Microsoft single sign-on for school accounts.
Data breachesWe will tell the school without undue delay, and within 48 hours of becoming aware of a breach affecting its data, with what we know and what we're doing.
When a licence endsWe delete or return the school's data within 90 days, or sooner if the school asks.
Risk assessments (DPIA)We'll help with your data protection impact assessment and answer your data protection officer's questions.
SafeguardingNo contact between users, AI limited to maths, concerns acted on the same day. See Safeguarding and child safety.
Data protection officer or IT lead? Email support@mathsforensics.com with the subject School data protection pack. We'll send the data processing agreement and this information, and answer your questions before any pilot starts.

Requests and contact

  • See, correct or delete data, or object to how it's used: email support@mathsforensics.com. We reply within one month. Parents can make requests for their child.
  • For school accounts, we pass requests to the school, as it's responsible for that data, and help it answer.
  • A safeguarding concern? Email the same address with Safeguarding in the subject. We act on it the same day.
  • Complaints: please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also contact the Spanish data protection authority, our lead regulator (aepd.es), or in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
