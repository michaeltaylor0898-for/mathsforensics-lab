Free KS3 maths revision sheets for Years 7, 8 and 9.

Printable worksheets written for parents helping at home. Every sheet has worked examples to learn from, three levels of practice (Developing, Securing and Extending) and a full answer key with the mistakes to look out for. New sheets are added regularly.

How to use a sheet

1. Learn it togetherRead the first page and talk through the worked examples.
2. Start at DevelopingLet your child work on their own. Most of a level right? Move up.
3. Check the answersThe answer key is at the back, with the common mistakes.
4. Ask how, not just whatGet them to explain their working: that's where the learning shows.
