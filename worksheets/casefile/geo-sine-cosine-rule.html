Case briefing

What you need to know

Label the sides with lower-case letters opposite their angles: side a is opposite angle A.

Sine rule (a pair of opposite side and angle, plus one more): asin A = bsin B = csin C. Cosine rule (two sides and the angle between, or all three sides): a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos A. Area = ½ab sin C.

  1. Label the triangle: angles A, B, C; sides a, b, c opposite them.
  2. Have you got a side and its opposite angle? Sine rule. Otherwise, cosine rule.
  3. Substitute, then rearrange before you press any buttons.
  4. Round only at the end, and check the biggest side is opposite the biggest angle.

WORKED EXAMPLEIn triangle PQR, PQ = 9 cm, PR = 7 cm and angle P = 50°. Find QR.

Solution

1Two sides and the angle between them, with no opposite pair: cosine rule.
2QR² = 9² + 7² − 2 × 9 × 7 × cos 50°.
3= 81 + 49 − 126 × 0.6428 = 49.01.
4QR = √49.01 = 7.00 cm. Check: it's opposite 50°, between the other two sides ✓
✓ QR = 7.00 cm (3 s.f.)
