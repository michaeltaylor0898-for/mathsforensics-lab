Case briefing

What you need to know

A multiplier turns any percentage change into one calculation. Increase by 15%: × 1.15. Decrease by 15%: × 0.85.

Percentage change = change ÷ original × 100. Reverse percentage: divide by the multiplier to get back to the original. Compound change: multiply by the multiplier once for every year, so original × multipliern.

  1. Decide: percentage of, change, reverse, or repeated change?
  2. Write the multiplier (100% ± the change, as a decimal).
  3. Multiply, divide, or use a power.
  4. Check: does the answer make sense? A price after an increase should be bigger.

WORKED EXAMPLEAfter a 20% price rise, a forensic camera costs £96. What was the price before the rise?

Solution

1A 20% rise means the new price is 120% of the original: multiplier 1.2.
2Original × 1.2 = £96.
3Original = £96 ÷ 1.2 = £80.
4Check: £80 × 1.2 = £96 ✓
✓ £80
