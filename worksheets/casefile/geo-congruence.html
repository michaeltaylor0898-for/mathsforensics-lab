Case briefing

What you need to know

Two triangles are congruent if they are exactly the same shape and size. You only need to show one of four conditions: SSS (three sides), SAS (two sides and the angle between them), ASA (two angles and a corresponding side) or RHS (right angle, hypotenuse, side).

AAA is not enough: it proves similar, not congruent. A congruence proof is a short list of three facts, each with a reason, then the condition.

  1. Name the two triangles, with the letters in matching order.
  2. Find three pairs of equal sides or angles.
  3. Give a reason for each: 'opposite sides of a parallelogram', 'common side', 'radii'.
  4. State the condition (SSS, SAS, ASA or RHS), then what follows from it.

WORKED EXAMPLEABCD is a parallelogram. Prove that triangles ABC and CDA are congruent.

Solution

1AB = CD (opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal).
2BC = DA (opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal).
3AC = CA (common side).
4So triangles ABC and CDA are congruent (SSS).
✓ Congruent by SSS
