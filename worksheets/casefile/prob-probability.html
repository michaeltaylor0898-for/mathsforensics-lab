Case briefing

What you need to know

Probabilities add up to 1. P(not A) = 1 − P(A). For independent events, multiply: P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B). For outcomes that can't happen together, add: P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B).

On a tree diagram, multiply along the branches and add between the routes you want. Without replacement, the second set of branches changes: one fewer item, and one fewer of the colour you took.

  1. Write the probabilities on every branch. Each set of branches adds up to 1.
  2. Multiply along each route you want.
  3. Add the routes together.
  4. For 'at least one', it's usually quicker to do 1 − P(none).

WORKED EXAMPLEA bag has 3 red and 5 blue counters. Two are taken without replacement. Find the probability that both are red.

Solution

1First counter: P(red) = 38.
2Without replacement there are now 2 red out of 7: P(red) = 27.
3Multiply along the branch: 38 × 27 = 656.
4Simplify: 328. Check: it's smaller than 38 × 38, as it should be ✓
✓ 328
