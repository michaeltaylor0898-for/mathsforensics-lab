Case briefing

What you need to know

Speed = distancetime. Rearranged: distance = speed × time, and time = distancespeed. The units must match: km and hours give km/h; metres and seconds give m/s.

On a distance-time graph, the gradient is the speed and a flat section means stopped. On a speed-time graph, the gradient is the acceleration and the area underneath is the distance travelled.

  1. Write the formula you need, rearranged for the unknown.
  2. Check the units: convert minutes to hours, or km to m, before you calculate.
  3. For a graph: read values carefully at grid lines, then use gradient or area.
  4. Give units with every answer, and turn decimal hours into minutes if asked.

WORKED EXAMPLEA patrol car drives 45 km in 36 minutes. Find its average speed in km/h.

Solution

1The answer is in km/h, so change the time to hours: 36 minutes = 3660 = 0.6 hours.
2Speed = distancetime = 450.6.
3= 75 km/h.
4Check: 75 km/h × 0.6 h = 45 km ✓
✓ 75 km/h

Case closed: Speed and distance-time graphs

  • Use speed = distance ÷ time in all three arrangements.
  • Convert between hours and minutes, and between km/h and m/s.
  • Read speed from the gradient of a distance-time graph.
  • Find distance from the area under a speed-time graph, and acceleration from its gradient.
  • Use density and pressure with the same formula triangle idea.
