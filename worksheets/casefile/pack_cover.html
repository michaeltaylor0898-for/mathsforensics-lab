How to use this pack

Every case file follows the same order: a case briefing with a worked example, then a Knowledge Check in three levels, then the solutions. Work through one topic at a time, with a pencil and a calculator (except where a question says not to).

SecuringGrades 4 to 5. The method, step by step.
ExtendingGrades 6 to 7. A step is hidden, a context to unpick, and one piece of tampered evidence.
MasteryGrades 8 to 9. No hints: plan before you calculate.
Mark your evidence. When you check your answers, write a letter next to every mark you lost:
R · ReadingMisread or missed part of the question
M · MethodDidn't know how to do it
A · AccuracyKnew how, slipped in the working

Mostly R or A? The fix is exam technique, not more practice of the topic. In 280 GCSE maths scripts we analysed, nearly one in three lost marks was avoidable in exactly this way.

Mastery solutions are in the Lab: scan the QR code on each sheet, write or photograph your working, and Forensic Marking marks it mark by mark, naming the mistake behind every lost mark. Your first marked question each week is free.

For parents: you don't need to know the maths. Ask your child which level they reached on each sheet, and which letter (R, M or A) came up most. That one letter tells you what to work on.
