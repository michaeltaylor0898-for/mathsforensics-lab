Case briefing

What you need to know

The graph of y = ax² + bx + c is a U shape (a ∩ if a is negative). The roots are where it crosses the x-axis: solve y = 0. The y-intercept is (0, c).

The turning point sits on the line of symmetry, halfway between the roots. Completing the square gives it directly: y = (x − p)² + q has turning point (p, q).

  1. Roots: factorise and solve = 0 (or read where the curve crosses the x-axis).
  2. y-intercept: put x = 0.
  3. Turning point: halfway between the roots, or complete the square.
  4. To solve an equation with a graph, rearrange it so one side is the curve, and draw the other side as a line.

WORKED EXAMPLEFind the roots, the y-intercept and the turning point of y = x² − 6x + 5.

Solution

1Roots: (x − 1)(x − 5) = 0, so x = 1 and x = 5.
2y-intercept: when x = 0, y = 5, so (0, 5).
3Line of symmetry halfway between the roots: x = 3.
4Turning point: y = 9 − 18 + 5 = −4, so (3, −4). Check: (x − 3)² − 4 = x² − 6x + 5 ✓
✓ Roots 1 and 5, y-intercept (0, 5), turning point (3, −4)
