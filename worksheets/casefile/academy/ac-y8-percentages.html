Learn it

What you need to know

Per cent means out of 100. 35% = 35100 = 0.35.

Without a calculator, build from easy percentages: 10% = ÷ 10, 1% = ÷ 100, 50% = ÷ 2, 25% = ÷ 4, 5% = half of 10%.

With a calculator, multiply by the decimal: 35% of £60 = 0.35 × 60.

Increase by 20%: find 20% and add it on, or multiply by 1.2. Decrease by 20%: take it off, or multiply by 0.8.

WORKED EXAMPLEFind 35% of £60 without a calculator.

Solution

110% = £60 ÷ 10 = £6.
230% = 3 × £6 = £18.
35% = half of 10% = £3.
435% = £18 + £3 = £21.
✓ £21

WORKED EXAMPLEIncrease £80 by 15%.

Solution

110% = £8 and 5% = £4, so 15% = £12.
2Add it on: £80 + £12 = £92.
3Or with a calculator: £80 × 1.15 = £92.
✓ £92

WORKED EXAMPLEWrite 18 out of 24 as a percentage.

Solution

1Write it as a fraction: 1824.
2Simplify: 1824 = 34.
334 = 75%. (Or 18 ÷ 24 × 100 = 75.)
✓ 75%

Mistakes to look out for

  • Stopping at the percentage: the discount is £13.50, but the sale price is £31.50.
  • Adding the number instead of the percentage: £60 increased by 5% is not £65.
  • Dividing by the new amount when finding a percentage change.
  • Thinking 10% up then 10% down gets back to where you started.
