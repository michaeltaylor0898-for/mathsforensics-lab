In this pack

1Rounding and estimating
Round to tens, hundreds, decimal places and significant figures, then use rounding to estimate answers and check calculations.
2Arithmetic with decimals
Add, subtract, multiply and divide with decimals, without a calculator, and know where the decimal point goes every time.
3Arithmetic with fractions
Add, subtract, multiply and divide fractions and mixed numbers, and find a fraction of an amount.
4Ordering fractions and decimals
Compare and order decimals, fractions and percentages, including negative numbers, using place value and common denominators.
5Ratio
Simplify ratios, share amounts in a ratio, and use ratio in recipes, mixtures and problems.
6Percentages
Find percentages of amounts with and without a calculator, increase and decrease by a percentage, and write one number as a percentage of another.
7Collecting like terms
Simplify expressions by collecting like terms, including negative terms and squared terms, and use them for perimeters.
8Expanding brackets and factorising
Multiply out brackets, expand and simplify, and factorise expressions by taking out the highest common factor.

How to use it

One sheet at a time

Pick the topic your child finds hardest, or the one their class is on now. Each sheet takes about 40 minutes and can be split over two evenings.

Learn it, then try it

Read the Learn it page together and talk through the worked examples. Then let your child work on their own, starting at Developing.

Move up a level

Most of a level right? Move up. Developing is the basics, Securing is the Year 8 standard, Extending is a stretch.

Check together

The answer key is at the back of each sheet, with the mistakes to look out for. Ask your child to explain their working, not just the answer.
