Case briefing

What you need to know

y = sin x and y = cos x are waves between −1 and 1 that repeat every 360°. y = tan x repeats every 180° and has asymptotes at 90°, 270°…

Your calculator gives one angle. The graph's symmetry gives the others: for sin, the second angle is 180° − x; for cos, it's 360° − x; for tan, add 180°.

  1. Sketch the graph for the range you're given.
  2. Use your calculator (or exact values) for the first angle.
  3. Use the symmetry of the graph to find every other angle in the range.
  4. Check each answer by substituting it back.

WORKED EXAMPLESolve sin x = −0.5 for 0° ≤ x ≤ 360°.

Solution

1sin⁻¹(−0.5) = −30°, which is outside the range.
2sin 30° = 0.5, and the sine graph is negative between 180° and 360°.
3By symmetry the angles are 180° + 30° and 360° − 30°.
4Check: sin 210° = −0.5 ✓ and sin 330° = −0.5 ✓
✓ x = 210° or 330°
