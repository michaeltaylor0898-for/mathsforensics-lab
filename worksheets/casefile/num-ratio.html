Case briefing

What you need to know

A ratio compares quantities of the same kind, in the same units. 3 : 5 means for every 3 of the first there are 5 of the second: 8 parts altogether.

Almost every ratio question comes down to finding the value of one part. Work out how many parts you know about, and what they're worth.

  1. Add the numbers in the ratio to find the total number of parts.
  2. Divide the amount you know by the number of parts it covers: that's one part.
  3. Multiply one part by each number in the ratio.
  4. Check: the shares add up to the total.

WORKED EXAMPLETwo suspects split £27,000 in the ratio 4 : 5. How much does each receive?

Solution

14 + 5 = 9 parts altogether.
2£27,000 ÷ 9 = £3,000 for one part.
3Suspect 1: 4 × £3,000 = £12,000. Suspect 2: 5 × £3,000 = £15,000.
4Check: £12,000 + £15,000 = £27,000 ✓
✓ £12,000 and £15,000
