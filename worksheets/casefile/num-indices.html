Case briefing

What you need to know

When you multiply powers of the same base, add the indices. When you divide, subtract them. A power of a power: multiply them.

A negative index means one over: a−n = 1 ÷ an. A fractional index means a root: a1/n is the nth root, and am/n is the nth root, raised to the power m. Anything to the power 0 is 1.

  1. Deal with numbers and letters separately.
  2. Apply one law at a time, writing each step.
  3. For a fractional index, take the root first: the numbers stay small.
  4. For an index equation, write both sides as powers of the same base, then set the indices equal.

WORKED EXAMPLEEvaluate 64−2/3.

Solution

1The negative means one over: 64−2/3 = 1 ÷ 642/3.
2The denominator 3 means cube root: ∛64 = 4.
3The numerator 2 means square it: 4² = 16.
4So the answer is 1 ÷ 16.
✓ 1/16
